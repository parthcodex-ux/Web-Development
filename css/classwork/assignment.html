About MSOT

Mirai School of Technology is a technology-focused institution offering industry-oriented undergraduate programs, including B.Tech in AI & ML. It emphasizes practical learning, coding, projects, and exposure to emerging technologies. The school aims to prepare students with the technical and problem-solving skills needed for careers in the technology industry.

Clubs of MSOT

  • Coding & Tech Club - Coding, AI/ML, web development, and technical projects.
  • Entrepreneurship Club -Startups, business ideas, innovation, and pitching.
  • Sports Club - Sports activities, competitions, and fitness.
